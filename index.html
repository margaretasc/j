This is simply something I feel needs to be said.

I remember that February night in 2025 when I first saw you. You were the last person I scrolled past before going to sleep, and it somehow stuck with me. Even talking to you back then felt different, quite strange. We weren’t even really talking, but something felt distinct about you. A quiet sense of knowing. Life got busy, you were still just a stranger, and I wasn’t great at replying. But somehow I always knew you’d message again. I was certain you wouldn’t disappear and yet equally sure you’d remain just a stranger. Then July came, and everything in my life shifted. I was more open, more receptive. And that’s when you reappeared. I’ve always been open with my friends about what’s going on in my life, but with you it was different. I told no one. And because of that, I was completely myself - unfiltered, unafraid. I simply enjoyed your presence, our little conversations. Then we met for the first time. And I felt relief. There was a click - quiet, calm. Unlike anything before. I was so lost in that feeling I could barely focus on what you were saying for the first fifteen minutes. I went home that day feeling peacefully happy. The second time we met, same calmness. But when you came closer, something inside me shifted. I just knew. No words for it. The next day I respected your decision to keep your distance. I had no choice, haha. But deep down I always knew you’d come back. Still, I was left with this unknown feeling. I did nothing but tell myself it will pass. But you always came back. And eventually I realised they were reminders. The strangest part? I felt you before you texted me. Every single time. An intuition so strong it scared even my friends. I’d say your name out loud and then your message would arrive. I tried so hard to move past it. I tried everything - isolating myself, exhausting myself, running 72 kilometres on a random Tuesday, making art, going back to old hobbies. Nothing worked. When I was on a trip in January, I ended up talking to a pilot on a plane, and it just hit me - I was nowhere near forgetting you. Through all of it, I grew. A lot. I know technically not much happened between us. But honestly it doesn’t matter whether I’m right or wrong about any of this - what matters is that I felt it. And what I felt was real. Despite my fear of connections like this. I still saw you as a potential friend. It was never about specific traits. It was simply you - something deeper, something I recognised without being able to explain it. Not potential. Something real. Something singular. For six months I respected your need for distance, and still, everything I felt remained. When I saw you again in February, I felt peace. Like coming home. It wasn’t about what you said or did, although I was quietly amazed you remembered the smallest details about me. It was just that feeling again. Calm. Certain. There are still so many things I haven’t told you, but those are details. This felt important to say. I’m not sure where I was going with this. It just needed to be said. I’m fully aware of where I stand with you - I’ve heard you say it, I remember, I know - so this is really just me getting it off my chest. And something oddly specific is you look like my grandfather did when he was around your age. I didn’t even realise it until months later.
